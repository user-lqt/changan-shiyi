Interior of a Tang dynasty lakeside banquet pavilion at Qujiang Pool, Chang'an, in the spring evening of the year 800, prepared for a poetry contest among newly graduated jinshi scholars. Seen from a seated guest's eye level across a long low lacquered wooden table in the foreground centre, its top empty and clear (no objects in the middle third of the table). Behind it, open red-lacquered columns and rolled bamboo blinds frame a view of the lake at dusk with willows, a curved bridge, floating lanterns on the water and the silhouette of the Big Wild Goose Pagoda far away. Scholars in pale green and blue robes sit at side tables in soft focus, wine cups floating on a small meandering stream (liushang qushui), apricot blossoms drifting. Warm lantern light, gentle depth of field, cinematic, calm and festive.
